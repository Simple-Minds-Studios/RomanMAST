What am I looking at?

A foreground object can bend and magnify light from a background star. These panels show how the published brightness measurements change over time. Higher points mean brighter measurements; each panel has its own magnitude scale.

The example uses published observations of OGLE-2016-BLG-1093. It does not simulate a new event or fit a planet model. Turn on the published error bars, compare the observing windows and inspect the source methods.

On smaller screens, swipe horizontally across a chart to read its axes.

Magnitude increases downward: brighter measurements appear higher. Each panel chooses its own vertical range. A shared time origin does not establish a shared detailed time scale or photometric zero point. Points and error bars are published data, not a new model or calibrated joint fit. No file is uploaded or sent by this viewer.
